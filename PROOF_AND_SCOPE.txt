TARGET AND SCOPE
For every nonabelian group G with g^4 = 1 for all g, the following are equivalent:
(1) G admits a simple undirected Cayley graph of chromatic number exactly three.
(2) G has a section isomorphic to D8, the dihedral group of order eight.
(3) G is not isomorphic to Q8 x E for any elementary abelian two-group E.
Groups and elementary abelian factors may be infinite. Graphs need not be
connected. Every positive case has an inverse-closed identity-free connection
set of cardinality at most ten. The negative assertion allows infinite sets.
The ambient set theory includes choice.

HUMAN PROOF
A five-cycle in the standard three-chromatic D8 Cayley graph lifts to any
D8 section, giving at most ten connection elements after taking inverses.
Without a D8 section, involutions are central; squares and commutators
are central; each noncommuting pair has common nontrivial square equal
to its commutator. A quaternion subgroup and a central elementary abelian
complement give Q8 x E. Every undirected Cayley graph of Q8 x E is
isomorphic to an elementary abelian Cayley graph, so Payan's obstruction
and a finite odd-cycle reduction exclude chromatic number three.

FORMAL PROOF
The complete formal proof is checked on Lean/Mathlib 4.29.0. It includes
the structural theorem, the quaternionic coordinate transfer, the cubelike
obstruction for arbitrary groups of exponent two, and the ten-element bound.
Its cubelike proof uses a direct combinatorial winding argument.

PRIORITY
The question is Krebs and Sankar, arXiv:2410.11028v2, Question 5.3.
The same D8-section criterion is stated conditionally in the public
tysunseven/CayleyExpanded project, which also verifies the identical D8
witness and section transfer. Goldstone-Weld (2010) identify the same
quaternionic family through a different graph symmetry property.
McCabe-Weld (2011), Proposition 4.10, supplies the splitting after central
involutions and a shared square have been established.
The manuscript acknowledges these results and presents the direct
unconditional argument and uniform bound. This record makes no claim of
first publication, independent discovery, or journal acceptance.
